FOR JUDGES

Judge's Quick Start Guide

Follow these steps to experience OvoWorks end-to-end in under 5 minutes.

1
Connect Your Wallet
Click "Connect Wallet" in the top right. Use MetaMask on SoDEX testnet. Your wallet address unlocks your personal trade history and portfolio view.
2
Claim Testnet Tokens
Visit the SoDEX faucet to claim free vUSDC test tokens. You need at least $10 vUSDC to execute a test trade through OvoWorks.
3
Launch the Copilot
Click "Launch App" to open OvoWorks. Ask it anything — "What are institutions doing with BTC?" or click "Market brief" to see live data in action.
4
Generate a Signal
Click the ⚡ Live Signal button. OvoWorks pulls live SoSoValue ETF flows + CoinGecko prices and generates a BUY/SELL/HOLD signal with confidence scoring.
5
Execute a Trade
From the signal panel, select BTC, set $10 amount, choose Spot or Leverage, and click BUY/LONG. The trade executes on SoDEX testnet via EIP-712 signing.
6
Verify On-Chain
Every trade is verifiable on ValueChain testnet explorer. Search wallet 0x95Cc...680fe to see all OvoWorks executed orders with their order IDs.
